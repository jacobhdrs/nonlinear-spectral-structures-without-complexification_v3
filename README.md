# Open Issues for Future Revision
(1) Set-theoretic representation of the pre-structure. The pre-structure Π_{n,a} is represented as a subset of Sₘ, which presupposes that the elements of Sₘ already exist as a collection. Under this reading, determining a structure from Π_{n,a} can only be understood as a selection from a pre-existing range, whereas the intended meaning is that the relevant relations and the admissible range are identified as a whole once (n, a) is given. The relation between Π_{n,a} and pst_n(Π_{n,a}), in particular whether pst_n(Π_{n,a}) ∈ Π_{n,a}, is therefore not formally specified in this version. In a future revision, we plan to define the pre-structure directly from the governing equation, after examining its algebraic form and results.

(2) Dependence of Axiom 3-3 and Corollary 2.4. Axiom 3-3 and Corollary 2.4 refer to a hypothetical construction in which the structure (or structural torque) of one stage is mapped into that of another by a stage mapping. The formal meaning of "a pre-structure determined by a structure" is left open, and these statements will be revised together with the direct definition of the pre-structure. The definitional statements of Section 2.4.2 will be strengthened in the same revision.

(3) Foundational framework. The framework is formulated within ZFC, with spectral structures as relational structures (g, R) on M. The formal status of the primitive relations Red, Der and of the equivalence ∼ is fixed only through the axioms and is not further analyzed here.
